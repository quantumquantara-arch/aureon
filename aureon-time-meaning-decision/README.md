# Aureon: Time–Meaning–Decision (TMD)

Aureon TMD is a formal structure that unifies:

* time
* meaning
* decision-making

into a single causal ledger.

It transforms action into something that can be inspected, replayed, validated, and trusted without interpretation or ambiguity.
No hidden state. No retroactive edits. No probabilistic guessing.

---

## Core Guarantees

**Temporal Integrity** — time is monotonic and append-only; nothing moves backward.
**Meaning Fixation** — meaning attaches at the moment of observation and cannot drift.
**Causal Coherence** — decisions reference structured anchors, not heuristics.
**Deterministic Replay** — any outcome can be reconstructed exactly.
**Transparent Auditability** — every link in the chain is traceable.

Together these create a system where reasoning becomes a physical object: stable, examinable, and reproducible.

---

## Motivation

Contemporary AI systems produce decisions that are:

* non-deterministic
* semantically unstable
* difficult to justify
* prone to drift over time
* impossible to reconstruct exactly

TMD provides the missing architecture: a causal substrate where every observation, interpretation, and action is bound to time and meaning in a way that cannot be rewritten.

This enables a new class of intelligence built on **causal truth rather than statistical correlation**.

## What TMD Makes Possible

An intelligence system built on TMD can:

* reproduce its reasoning identically across time and hardware
* justify every action with immutable causal anchors
* prevent meaning drift by fixing semantics at observation
* operate without “hidden layers” or untraceable transformations
* produce an audit trail that is complete, minimal, and incorruptible

This changes the philosophical and technical basis of machine reasoning:
from inference → to causation,
from opacity → to structure,
from probability → to proof.

TMD reframes intelligence as a **time-bound, meaning-bound physical process**, not a statistical artifact.
