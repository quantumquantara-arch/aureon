# Evercycle Codex — 108 Human Archetypes
Canonical human-cycle archetypes corresponding to each personal phase (1–108).
These archetypes describe psychological state, energetic expression, internal tasks, and likely behaviors.

Archetypes are grouped into the five arcs:
- Dissolution (1–22)
- Reconstruction (23–45)
- Stabilization (46–68)
- Expansion (69–90)
- Integration (91–108)
