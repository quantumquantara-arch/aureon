# The Evercycle Codex
A temporal architecture for Aureon, NexLevelAI, and the Quantara ecosystem

The Evercycle Codex is a unified map of cyclical time that spans three layers of reality:

1. **Cosmic cycles** — long-wave astronomical, climatic, and environmental rhythms  
2. **Civilizational cycles** — the recurring six-phase rise/fall pattern of societies  
3. **Human cycles** — the 108-phase Continuity Cycle of psychological development  

The Codex is not prophecy and not mysticism.  
It is a structural language for recognizing recurring temporal patterns and understanding *where* a person, a system, or a civilization sits within its current arc.

It provides a shared temporal grammar across the entire Quantara stack, enabling coherent time-based reasoning inside Aureon, NexLevelAI, Veyn, and AEI systems.


## Why it matters
Most AI systems understand data but not time.  
They react, but cannot truly contextualize.

The Evercycle Codex provides:

- A common vocabulary for “what kind of moment this is”
- A temporal backbone for long-range forecasting and ethics
- A coherence-based lens (κ, τ, Σ) for evaluating risk and stability
- A multi-scale map that connects human experience to civilizational and cosmic arcs

It allows AI to understand not just **events**, but **eras**.


## The Three Strata

### 1. Cosmic — The 12 Aeonic Phases  
Derived from precessional cycles, climate patterns, and archaeological expansions.  
Represents long-wave conditions that shape civilizations.

Variable: `aeon_phase ∈ {1..12}`

---

### 2. Civilizational — The 6-Wave Societal Cycle  
A recurring pattern observed across cultures:

1. Initiation  
2. Expansion  
3. Tension  
4. Crisis  
5. Reorganization  
6. Stabilization  

Variable: `civilizational_phase ∈ {1..6}`  
Fractional phases supported.

---

### 3. Human — The 108-Phase Continuity Cycle  
A fine-grained map of psychological states organized into five arcs:

- Dissolution  
- Reconstruction  
- Stabilization  
- Expansion  
- Integration  

Variable: `personal_phase ∈ {1..108}`

Movement is nonlinear and context-dependent.


## Coherence Metrics: κ, τ, Σ

The Codex uses three state metrics across all scales:

- **κ — Coherence:** internal integration  
- **τ — Temporal Alignment:** fit with the current phase  
- **Σ — Systemic Risk:** fragmentation, vulnerability  

These metrics help systems evaluate stability, phase-fit, and tipping-point likelihood.


## The Evermap
A conceptual visualization consisting of:

- The Aeonic Wheel (12)
- The Civilizational Wave (6)
- A coherence heatmap (κ, τ, Σ)
- The individual’s Continuity Marker (1–108)

This map allows humans and AI to see nested time layers at once.


## Integration in Aureon
Aureon uses the Codex to:

- Model user psychological trajectories (108 phases)  
- Adjust tone, pacing, and depth based on κ, τ, Σ  
- Contextualize distress within civilizational arcs  
- Maintain temporal coherence in long-form memory  

User agency is always prioritized.
