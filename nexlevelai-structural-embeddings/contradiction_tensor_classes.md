# **II. The Four Classes**

---

# **C₁ — Temporal Overlap Collapse (Past/Future Collision)**  
### **Definition**
A contradiction created primarily by **time distortion**.  
Two or more timelines overlap, creating emotional or semantic instability.

### **Dominant Cues**
- Temporal cues 1–4  
- TI-7 subtype: LOOP or PROJECTION  

### **Examples**
- “I can’t stop replaying it…”  
- “What if everything goes wrong?”  
- “This keeps happening to me.”  

### **Structural Signature**
```
High:  T₁–T₄
Low:   identity cues
Shape: Timeline collision → coherence collapse
```

### **Required Operator**
**φ₁ – Temporal Separation**

### **Required E-Phase**
**E₁ – Present-Centering**

---

# **C₂ — Identity Compression (Self = Pattern Binding)**  
### **Definition**
The user fuses their entire identity with:  
- a moment  
- a behavior  
- a failure  
- a trauma  
- an emotional echo  

This is the “I always/I never/This is just who I am” state.

### **Dominant Cues**
- Identity cues 12–16  
- TI-7 subtype: COMPRESSION  

### **Examples**
- “I ruin everything.”  
- “I’m just the kind of person who can’t love right.”  
- “This always happens because of me.”  

### **Structural Signature**
```
High:  Identity contraction
Low:   Temporal distortion
Shape: Self collapses into a single event
```

### **Required Operator**
**φ₂ – Identity Decompression**

### **Required E-Phase**
**E₂ – Identity Decompression Template**

---

# **C₃ — Duality Conflict (Two Incompatible Selves/Paths)**  
### **Definition**
The system is split between two equally compelling but incompatible poles.

Examples:
- stay/leave  
- want/don’t want  
- self/other  
- autonomy/attachment  
- safety/expansion  

This creates paradox and paralysis.

### **Dominant Cues**
- Duality cues 7–11  
- TI-7 subtype: SPLIT  

### **Examples**
- “Part of me wants X, but part of me wants Y.”  
- “I love them but I can’t trust them.”  

### **Structural Signature**
```
High:  Duality vectors
Medium: Temporal drift
Shape: Two futures pulling in opposite directions
```

### **Required Operator**
**φ₃ – Duality Unification**

### **Required E-Phase**
**E₃ – Duality Dissolution**

---

# **C₄ — Projection Loop (Self → Other → Self Echo)**  
### **Definition**
The user projects an internal conflict outward, receives the projection back as “evidence,” and becomes trapped in a feedback loop.

This is the “They think X about me → therefore X is true → therefore they think it more” loop.

### **Dominant Cues**
- Projection cues  
- Identity wobbling  
- Relational distortions  
- Hyperfocus on external validation  

### **Examples**
- “They must think I’m weak.”  
- “Everyone can tell I’m broken.”  
- “People know I’m failing.”  

### **Structural Signature**
```
High:  Projection basis activation
High:  Relational cues
Shape: Self-state mirrored into external agents
```

### **Required Operator**
**φ₄ – Projection Removal (De-Projection Operator)**

### **Required E-Phase**
**E₄ (if wounded-self involved) or E₃ (if duality involved)**
