# Zero-Point Coherence Map  
### Mapping d(V, Z₀) to Cognitive Stability Zones (Green / Yellow / Red)

The distance metric **d(V, Z₀)** measures how far the user’s active cognitive-temporal vector **V** has moved away from the Zero-Point Coherence State **Z₀** (present-time, contradiction-free, unified).

This produces the 3-Zone Coherence Model used throughout NextLevelAI.

---

## **1. Coherence Zone (Green Zone)**
### **d(V, Z₀) < 0.4**

**Characteristics**
- Present-time orientation intact  
- Minimal temporal distortion  
- No contradictions forming  
- Identity continuity stable  
- Minimal φ-phase correction required  

**System Behavior**
- Respond normally  
- Provide light grounding only if necessary  
- Maintain flow  
- No major reframing  

---

## **2. Transitional Zone (Yellow Zone)**
### **0.4 ≤ d(V, Z₀) < 1.0**

**Characteristics**
- Contradictions beginning to form  
- Timeline drifting (future fear or past gravity)  
- Identity wobbling  
- Emotional charge rising  
- Requires structured φ-phase contradiction mapping  

**System Behavior**
- Slow the reasoning pace  
- Isolate contradictions  
- Apply structural reframing  
- Reorient gently toward stability  

---

## **3. Fragmentation Zone (Red Zone)**
### **d(V, Z₀) ≥ 1.0**

**Characteristics**
- Multiple distortions active simultaneously  
- Emotional overload  
- Identity fragmentation  
- Timeline collapse or compression  
- Temporally unstable vector **V**  
- Requires stabilizing e-phase templates  

**System Behavior**
- No forced logic  
- No hard reframes  
- Use soft grounding  
- Reduce temporal load  
- Restore stability before insight  

---

## **Mathematical Summary**

**Distance Function:**  
`d(V, Z₀) = ∥V − Z₀∥` (normalized)

Where:  
- **V** = the 16-cue active cognitive vector  
- **Z₀** = the zero-point coherence baseline  

**Zone Thresholds:**  
- **Green:** `d < 0.4`  
- **Yellow:** `0.4 ≤ d < 1.0`  
- **Red:** `d ≥ 1.0`
