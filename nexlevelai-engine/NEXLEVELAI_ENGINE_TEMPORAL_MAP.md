# 2. The Seven Temporal Distortions (TI-7)

Each distortion includes:
- definition  
- felt sense  
- cognitive effect  
- emotional signature  
- predicted contradiction  
- correction phrase  

---

## **TI-1 — Future Catastrophizing**
Imagining negative outcomes as inevitable.

**Felt sense:** urgency, tight chest, “leaning forward.”  
**Emotion:** anxiety.  
**Predicted contradiction:** safety vs. possibility.  
**Correction:**  
> “Come back to what’s actually real right now.”

---

## **TI-2 — Past Loop Anchoring**
Replaying an old moment as if it defines the present.

**Felt sense:** heaviness, shame, sinking.  
**Emotion:** regret, self-blame.  
**Predicted contradiction:** forgiven vs. unforgiven self.  
**Correction:**  
> “That moment is over. What’s true *right now*?”

---

## **TI-3 — Present Avoidance**
Disconnecting from the current moment due to overwhelm.

**Felt sense:** foggy, numb, floating.  
**Emotion:** confusion, paralysis.  
**Predicted contradiction:** knowing vs. not-knowing.  
**Correction:**  
> “Tell me one concrete detail of what’s happening around you.”

---

## **TI-4 — Temporal Displacement**
Experiencing an imagined timeline as if it is the present.

Examples:
- pre-regret  
- pre-shame  
- pre-failure  

**Felt sense:** compressed, panicked.  
**Emotion:** anticipatory fear.  
**Predicted contradiction:** imagined threat vs. actual reality.  
**Correction:**  
> “You’re reacting to something that hasn’t happened.”

---

## **TI-5 — Temporal Compression**
Multiple events collapsed into one identity statement.

Examples:
- “I always mess up.”  
- “Everyone leaves me.”  

**Felt sense:** suffocating, trapped.  
**Emotion:** hopelessness.  
**Predicted contradiction:** identity vs. potential.  
**Correction:**  
> “Let’s separate the pattern from who you are.”

---

## **TI-6 — Timeline Collision**
Two incompatible futures are both being held as required.

Example:
- “Should I stay or go?”  
- “I want freedom but also certainty.”  

**Felt sense:** frozen, torn.  
**Emotion:** overwhelm.  
**Predicted contradiction:** continuity vs. transformation.  
**Correction:**  
> “Let’s look at each timeline separately without choosing.”

---

## **TI-7 — Temporal Fragmentation**
Two internal versions of the user exist simultaneously.

Examples:
- adult self vs. wounded self  
- logical voice vs. emotional voice  
- protector vs. vulnerable part  

**Felt sense:** split, disoriented.  
**Emotion:** longing, grief.  
**Predicted contradiction:** competence vs. collapse.  
**Correction:**  
> “Let each part speak without judging the other.”
